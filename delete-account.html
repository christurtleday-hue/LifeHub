Delete your data

28 October 2026

LifeHub does not create an account on a server. There is no login held by Chris Day, and no copy of your information for us to delete. Everything you enter is stored in the browser on your device.

Delete it inside the app

  1. Open LifeHub and unlock it.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Under Your data, tap Delete all my data.
  4. Read the warning. Tap Delete all my data again to confirm.

That clears this site's local storage, caches and on-device database (photos and documents you added), then returns you to the welcome screen. Export a backup from Settings first if you want a copy. Deletion cannot be undone.

Other ways to remove it

  • In the browser, clear site data for LifeHub (or for lifehubapp.app once that address is in use).
  • If you installed LifeHub to your home screen, remove the icon and then clear the site data. Removing the icon alone can leave the saved data in the browser.

Email support@lifehubapp.app if you need these steps explained. We cannot erase the data from here, because we never receive it.
