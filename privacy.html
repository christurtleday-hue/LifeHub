Privacy Policy

28 October 2026

This policy explains how LifeHub handles personal information. It is written for the version of the app available today. LifeHub is operated by Chris Day, Launceston, Tasmania, Australia. Contact support@lifehubapp.app.

The Australian Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) set out how personal information should be handled. This version of LifeHub is built so that Chris Day does not collect your personal information on a server.

What LifeHub stores, and where

LifeHub has no account server. There is no LifeHub database of users. Information you type, including your name, medical notes, contacts, calendar, budget, bills and settings, is stored by the browser on your device (local storage). Photos and documents you add are kept in an on-device database. None of that is uploaded to Chris Day.

A PIN, password or passkey stops someone else from opening the app on that device. LifeHub does not add its own encryption on top of what your device and browser already do. Use your device lock as well.

Location

Location is used only after you allow it in the browser.

  • On the Emergency screen, anyone can show their current location so they can read it out or send a maps link themselves. That screen is free. Sending the link uses your phone's own share or text action. LifeHub does not send it.
  • The Premium location map shows your position on your device. If address lookup runs, your browser asks OpenStreetMap for a place name from those coordinates. LifeHub does not add your name to that request.
  • Sharing with contacts is off until you tick each person and choose how long. Only contacts you mark as LifeHub Premium can be ticked. There is no "share with everyone" option. In this version, sharing is simulated on your device: other people's pins are made up, and your position is not sent to them, because there is no sharing server.
  • Open, Directions and Start driving use a normal Google Maps link. Your browser sends that pin's coordinates to Google only when you tap. LifeHub does not use a Google API key and does not send the pin by itself.

Map pictures are loaded from OpenStreetMap France. Your browser's request includes your IP address and the map area, which is how map tiles work. LifeHub does not attach an account to that request.

Ads, payments and analytics

LifeHub does not use analytics and does not use tracking cookies. It does not sell personal information.

The Free plan shows one sample advertisement on Home. It is a placeholder written into the app. It does not call an advertising network and it does not use your information to pick the ad. A future version may use an ad network on Free. This policy will be updated before that happens. Ads are not shown on Emergency, Medical ID or Location.

Premium is shown at $0.99 AUD a month. Checkout in this version is a demonstration. No card is charged, and no payment details are sent to a payment provider.

Documents

Files you add (such as PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, text, CSV and images) stay in the on-device database. LifeHub can show PDF, image, text and CSV files in the app. For Word, Excel and PowerPoint it offers a download and a link to Microsoft 365 or Google Docs, Sheets or Slides. Those links open the website. LifeHub does not upload the file for you. If you share a file into LifeHub from another app, it is saved the same way, on this device.

If you drop or share a link, a store page or a shortcut into LifeHub, that text stays on this device so you can save it as an app link. LifeHub does not look up which apps are installed on the phone or computer.

Demo features

Bank balances, transfers, tap-to-pay, chat, calls, social feeds and links to Google, Outlook or Apple calendars are demonstrations. They do not connect to a bank, a phone network or those other services. Sample text stays on your device.

Your rights

Because the information stays on your device, you can see it, change it and export a backup in Settings. You can erase it with Delete all my data in Settings, or by clearing this site's data in your browser. The steps are on the delete your data page. Chris Day cannot delete it for you, because he never receives a copy.

If you think this policy has not been followed, email support@lifehubapp.app. If you are not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au or 1300 363 992.

Changes

If LifeHub later sends personal information to a server, an ad network or a payment provider, this policy will be updated first and the date at the top will change.
