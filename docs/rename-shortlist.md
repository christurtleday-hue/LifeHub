# LifeHub rename shortlist

Not a rename. LifeHub stays the product name until a later decision. These are candidates only, checked lightly for obvious clashes in November 2026.

1. **Dayknit** — A day pulled together. No well-known app of this name turned up. A small knitting brand uses similar wording, so the store listing would need a clear subtitle.
2. **Hearthly** — Home, people, and money in one place. hearthly.com is parked. Uncommon as an app name.
3. **Kinwell** — Family and everyday admin. kinwell.com belongs to a US health system (Kinwell). Avoid.
4. **Lumina Day** — A calmer daily home. Lumina is already used by several finance and photo apps. The two-word form is less crowded, still easy to confuse.
5. **Nookday** — A small place for the day. nookday.com looks unused. “Nook” is crowded (Barnes & Noble Nook, Nook app).
6. **Ordinary App** — Plain tools for an ordinary day. The word is generic, so a store search is noisy. ordinary.app was not an established product in a quick check.
7. **Pocketfort** — The private pocket for life admin. pocketfort.com looks unused. “Pocket” is a crowded prefix.
8. **Weekfold** — The week, folded into one list. No established app of this name turned up. weekfold.com looks unused.
9. **Homestead** — Too close to Apple Home, Google Home, and many “homestead” products. Skip.
10. **LifeOS** — Describes the job, and it is already used by personal-dashboard projects and a few small apps. Skip.

Safer short list if a rename happens later: Dayknit, Hearthly, Nookday, Weekfold. Kinwell, Homestead, and LifeOS are the obvious conflicts.
