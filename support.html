Support

27 October 2026

Email support@lifehubapp.app. LifeHub is looked after by Chris Day in Launceston, Tasmania, Australia.

Say which phone or browser you are using, and what you expected to happen. Do not send Medicare numbers, full card numbers or your LifeHub PIN. Those stay on your device, and email is not the right place for them.

What this version can and cannot do

  • Your notes, calendar, contacts and medical details stay on this device. There is no LifeHub server to sign in to.
  • Banking, transfers, tap-to-pay, chat, calls, social feeds, outside calendars and other people's map pins are demos. They are labelled in the app.
  • Premium checkout does not take a real payment.
  • To erase everything, use Settings, then Delete all my data. The steps are on the delete your data page. Support cannot delete it remotely, because it is not sent to us.

In an emergency, call 000. Do not wait for an email reply.
